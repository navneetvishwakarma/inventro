- **S-15** [#33](https://github.com/navneetvishwakarma/inventro/issues/33) — commit a purchase for an item whose previous predicted_depletion_at was already well in the past relative to the new purchase date; confirm rate_correction increases (clamped at 2.0 under repeated fires).
